# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PactAgent is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level
of technical expertise.

We value careful technical disagreement. Critique ideas, designs, and code without
attacking the people behind them.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a healthy community include:

- showing empathy, patience, and respect;
- giving and receiving constructive feedback;
- explaining trade-offs and assumptions clearly;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and repairing harm; and
- protecting other contributors' privacy and security.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome attention;
- insults, personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- encouraging unsafe handling of credentials, private keys, ecash tokens, or real
  funds; and
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  community.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
project events, and other spaces where someone is representing PactAgent. It also
applies when conduct outside project spaces has a clear impact on community safety.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive or unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers using
a contact method listed on the [repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/comwanga).
Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect the reporter's privacy as far as
reasonably possible, and avoid conflicts of interest. A maintainer involved in a
report will not take part in deciding its outcome.

Depending on severity and context, maintainers may issue a private correction or
warning, remove content, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a
participant. Serious threats may be escalated to the relevant platform or legal
authority when necessary to protect someone's safety.

Retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report or assists with an
investigation is prohibited.
